Mohjeta Picks Privacy Policy

Updated November 3, 2026. This policy covers Mohjeta Picks and its website, mohjeta.com.

Browsing and signing in

You can browse public picks without signing in. If you create an account, we use Supabase to handle sign-in and store your account ID, email, display name and, when supplied by Google or Discord sign-in, a profile picture link. Your display name and picture can appear with your activity. Your sign-in email is private.

Community and fantasy games

Chat messages and Pick'em picks and results can be seen by other visitors. Supabase stores this activity so people can participate and see results. Moderators can review reported messages and manage account restrictions.

If you save a fantasy team, we store its name and player selections with your account. Saved teams, public-round scores and public standings can be viewed by others. Joining a private league also creates a membership record. Its invitation code, member list, league scores and standings are available to league members. A team you enter in a private league still has a public team profile.

Seven-day trial and subscription access

When you start the website's seven-day trial, we record your account ID and start time to calculate when access ends and whether you have used the trial. Starting that website trial does not charge a payment card. For a subscription, we store the account ID, access end date, source of the access grant and the time it was updated. We use these records to unlock member features. If you follow a subscription link to a payment provider, that provider handles the checkout details you enter. Our membership database does not store your card number.

Notifications, settings and visits

If you enable alerts, we store your browser's push address, delivery keys and alert preferences. The subscription may be linked to your account. We use your browser's push service to deliver the alerts. Turn alerts off in More to remove that subscription.

Your browser stores preferences such as the age check and display settings. For visitor counts, we record a random browser ID and visit date in Supabase. Visitors see aggregate counts. The live online indicator can show a signed-in user's display name while they are connected. The site does not use advertising cookies. Our web host, GitHub Pages, may process IP addresses and other technical request data to deliver and secure the site.

Retention, deletion and contact

We retain account and activity records to provide sign-in, game history, membership access and moderation. To request deletion of your account and associated data, or to ask a privacy question, email mohjozojo@gmail.com from your sign-in address. Please do not send passwords or payment details. You can also clear locally saved preferences in your browser settings.
