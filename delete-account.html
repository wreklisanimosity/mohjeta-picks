Delete your Mohjeta account

You can delete your account in Mohjeta: sign in, open your account picture or name, choose Delete account, then confirm. This permanently removes your sign-in account and associated data.

Request deletion without the app

Email mohjozojo@gmail.com from your Mohjeta sign-in email with the subject “Mohjeta account deletion”. If you cannot access that email, contact the same address for help verifying ownership. You do not need to reinstall the app. Do not send passwords or payment card details.

Request account deletion by email

What is deleted

Your sign-in account, profile, messages, reports, Pick’em entries, saved fantasy team, private leagues you own, league memberships, account-linked alert subscriptions, trial and membership records are deleted from the live service. Deleting a league you own also removes that league’s membership and game records. Historical public game scores may remain with your name replaced by “Deleted account”. Random visitor counts are not linked to your account.

Cancel billing separately

Deleting Mohjeta does not cancel a Google Play subscription or issue a refund. To prevent future charges, manage or cancel your Google Play subscription. For purchases through another provider, cancel with that provider. Google and other payment providers keep their own transaction records under their policies.

Deletion removes live records. Any existing backup copies remain subject to the backup provider’s retention schedule and are not used for normal app access. Contact us about a deletion request or retained data at the address above.
